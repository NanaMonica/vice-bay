Vice Bay is a 3D open-world crime game (three.js, vendored under `game/vendor/`): drive around a fictional sun-soaked city at golden hour, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level rises — with the VBPD Air-1 "Kestrel" helicopter overhead and roadblocks at four stars — fight on foot, and run 10 story missions for Mara, from your first stolen car to the Bay King finale. Earn respect, tune your car at Sunset Garage, and flip between three radio stations (Bay 98.5 FM, Vice Wave 104.3, Palm Talk 88.1) as you cross the city's ten districts.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard: WASD to walk/drive, E to steal a car, F to fight, mouse click to shoot, R to change the radio, drag to orbit the camera, P to pause, M to mute, C to continue your save.

Built for make.fun's ["Make GTA 6" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) (gta6-01). All art, sound, city, characters and story are original — no assets from Rockstar Games.
