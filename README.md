Vice Bay is a 3D open-world crime game (three.js, vendored under `game/vendor/`): drive around a fictional sun-soaked city, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level rises, fight on foot, and run 5 story missions for Mara — from your first stolen car to the Sunset Getaway.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard: WASD to walk/drive, E to steal a car, F to fight, mouse click to shoot, drag to orbit the camera, P to pause, M to mute, C to continue your save.

Built for make.fun's ["Make GTA 6" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) (gta6-01). All art, sound, city, characters and story are original — no assets from Rockstar Games.
